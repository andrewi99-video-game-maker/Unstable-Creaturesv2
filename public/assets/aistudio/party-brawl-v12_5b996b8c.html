Every button takes you to the same get ready screen: pick your creature (each creature has its own name and only ONE player can be each creature), then hats, glasses, back items, colours and team.
Controls & weapons (tap to open)🎤 Allow the microphone for voice chat (proximity based; M mutes)
📱 Phone / tablet: drag on the LEFT half to move, drag on the RIGHT half to look around, tap the round buttons (hold L HAND + R HAND together to grab).
WASD move · Space jump · Left/Right mouse (or Z/X) = left/right hand · Q head-butt · E kick · 1 dance · 2 wave
Hold BOTH hands to grab an animal, a crate or a wall. Just grabbing drags them around; also hold SHIFT to lift them (or to climb a wall).
Knock creatures out, grab them and drop them off the map! Hits do more damage when you are running or falling into them.
Weapons are rare and the map changes every round! Walk over a weapon to pick it up and press F to use it. With a gun you face where the camera points: look up/down to aim. Aim the Vortex at the ground to launch yourself! Fell off an edge? Hold BOTH hands next to the cliff to grab it, then Shift to climb. You can still fight while someone is holding you. Weapons: 🦈 Shark Gun (shoots a shark), 🌀 Vortex Cannon (blasts air), 🍬 Candy Cane (hit people, swings side to side), 🪝 Grappling Hook (auto-aims at the creature closest to your crosshair, even a bit above/below, and reels it in to you), 🔨 Big Hammer (slow, huge hit), 💣 Bomb (throw it, goes off after ~2 seconds or when it touches someone), 🍌 Banana Peel (drop it, whoever steps on it slips), ⚫ Black Hole Blaster (one shot: press fire to launch the ball, press again to park it - 5 seconds later it drags in everything within a HUGE range for 5 seconds - you can still fight inside it! - then it pops and blasts everybody outward, off the map if you are unlucky), 🧊 Ice Machine (hold fire to spray ice; enough ice freezes someone solid, and they have to spam JUMP to break out).
🎮 Xbox controller: left stick move · right stick camera · A jump · LB / RB = left / right hand (hold both to grab) · LT lift · RT use weapon · X head-butt · B kick · Y dance · D-pad left wave
Free-for-all: last one standing scores a point. Team battle: last team standing scores (pick your team in the Team row of your card). First to 5 wins.
